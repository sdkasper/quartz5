## "As much as necessary, as little as possible."

### I help people cut through productivity noise and build simple workflows that fit their real lives.

LeanProductivity is about achieving better results with less effort by eliminating wasteful practices and focusing on value-added activities. Through my [YouTube channel](https://kspr.me/yt) and blog, I share tutorials, insights, and free resources on tools like Obsidian, workflow automation, and structured methodologies.

My approach is not about doing more - it’s about doing the right things efficiently.
