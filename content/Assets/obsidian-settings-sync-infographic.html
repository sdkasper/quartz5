The Process

4 Steps to Consistent Settings

STEP 1

Reset All

Clean slate across every device

  • Disable Settings Sync
  • Do this on ALL devices
  • Close Obsidian
STEP 2

Configure Primary

Set up your one source of truth

  • Open Obsidian
  • Set all preferences
  • Enable Settings Sync
  • Wait for full upload
STEP 3

Connect Others

Secondary devices pull settings

  • Close Obsidian
  • Delete .obsidian folder
  • Open Obsidian
  • Enable Settings Sync
STEP 4

Keep It Clean

Ongoing maintenance rules

  • Only change on primary
  • If conflict: redo Step 3
  • Keep Sync enabled
→
→
→
!

Order Matters

The primary device MUST finish uploading before you enable sync on any secondary device. If a secondary syncs first, it pushes its own settings to the cloud and overwrites your configuration.

Going Forward

Rules to Keep It Working

⚙

Settings Changes

Only change settings, themes, and hotkeys on your primary device.

✚

Plugin Installs

Install and configure plugins on the primary device only. They sync automatically.

↺

If Something Breaks

On the affected device: close Obsidian, delete .obsidian, reopen, enable sync.
