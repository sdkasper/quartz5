Local Backup Plugin

Obsidian community plugin. Creates zip archives on start, close, and hourly intervals. Limit retention to 5 days.

FreeFileSync

Free, open source. Differential backup (changes only) or mirror (exact replica including deletions). Windows, Mac, Linux.

Task Scheduler

Built-in on Windows. Schedule FreeFileSync batch jobs daily or weekly. Mac alternative: Launchd or Task Till Dawn.
