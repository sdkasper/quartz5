Lean Obsidian Terminal

Eliminating Context Friction
The Problem

Window Switching Kills Focus

Every alt-tab between Obsidian and a terminal breaks your flow. Multiply that by dozens of times a day and the friction adds up fast.

✗

Open in Terminal

Opens an external CMD window. Not inside Obsidian. Defeats the purpose.

✗

Terminal Plugin

Embeds a panel but mirrors to an external window. Resizing breaks it. Throws errors.

The Solution

A Real Terminal Inside Your Vault

Lean Terminal runs your actual system shell directly in Obsidian. No external windows. No mirroring. Full interactivity.
