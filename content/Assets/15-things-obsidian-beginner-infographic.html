From First Vault to Confident Note-Taker

No Coding Required

Obsidian has three view modes. Pick the one that fits your comfort level.

Editing View

Live preview with formatted text. Type Markdown, see results instantly. Best for most users.

Reading View

Clean, read-only display. No syntax visible. Perfect for reviewing finished notes.

Source Mode

Raw Markdown with full syntax visible. For those who want complete control.

Organize Your Notes
Properties, Tags & Aliases

Tags

Categorize notes across folders. Search and filter by topic regardless of where the note lives.

#tag inline or in frontmatter

Properties

Structured data fields in frontmatter. Track created dates, status, custom fields, and more.

status: draft

Aliases

Alternative names for notes. Search and link by alias without renaming the file.

aliases: [PKM, BASB]
