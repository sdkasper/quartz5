I just got the confirmation that I passed the Enterprise DNA certification for Power BI. Why do I mention this?

Several reasons, actually:

1. I am somewhat happy to have passed the certification.
2. Doing the certification helped me to improve my understanding of advanced Power BI methods including data modeling and visualization.
3. I want to share and commend all the work, Enterprise DNA – and Sam McKay – are doing around Power BI. If you have not done so, you should really take a look at their [website](http://enterprisedna.co), the tons of free videos on their [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCy2rBgj4M1tzK-urTZ28zcA), and then get a membership. For me, the investment was totally worth it and I am convinced it will be for you, if you want to (or have to) work with Power BI.
