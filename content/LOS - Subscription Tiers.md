# Tiers

| Tier | Daily AI Generations | Price |
|------|---------------------|-------|
| Explorer (Free) | 5 | $0 |
| Pro | 50 | $8/mo or $75/yr |
| Power | Unlimited | $12/mo or $108/yr |

Every advanced generation - i.e., generations that are not covered by the built-in logic - uses one credit.

Starter cards (pre-built templates) do not count against your daily limit - only AI chat and advanced wizard generations do.

All prices are in USD excl. taxes.
