# Feedback
![[LOS - Feedback.webp|Feedback form with Category dropdown, Title field, and Description area for bug reports|right]]
Click the `Feedback` button in the bottom-right corner of the app.

- Pick a **Category** - Bug Report, Feature Request, Performance Issue, or Other
- Enter a **Title** - short summary of your feedback (max 200 characters)
- **Description** - details, steps to reproduce, or your suggestion (max 2000 characters)

Your feedback is submitted as a GitHub issue and reviewed by the development team.

You must be signed in to submit feedback.

Your email address is partially masked in the submission for privacy.
