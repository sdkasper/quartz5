# Create Your Account

![[LOS - Getting Started.webp|Sign In dialog with email and password fields and Forgot password link|300|right]]

1. Go to https://kspr.me/los
2. Click `Sign Up` and enter your name, email, and password
3. Your password must meet all five rules:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one digit
   - At least one special character
4. Confirm your email by clicking the link in the confirmation message
5. Sign in with your credentials

# The User Interface
After signing in you see the main interface with three module tabs at the top: **Bases**, **CSS Snippets**, and **Templates**. Your tier badge and consumed daily generations show in the top-right corner.

![[LOS - Getting Started-1.webp|Lean Obsidian Studio main interface with Bases tab active, starter cards, and chat builder]]
## Quick Walkthrough
1. Click the **Bases** tab
2. Click the **Reading List** starter card
3. The output appears instantly with a validation badge, copy button, and download button
4. Copy the output or download the `.base` file
5. Follow [[LOS - Installing Generated Files]] to add it to your vault
