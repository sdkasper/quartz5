# FAQ

> [!question]- When does it go live?
> I don't have a fixed date for going live and will only release it when it works properly.

> [!question]- Do I need technical knowledge?
> No, everything works without technical knowledge. That's kind of the point. You need to know how to download or copy/paste the created code into the right place.

> [!question]- How is this different from asking ChatGPT or any other AI?
> Lean Obsidian Studio is purpose-built for Obsidian. It knows the exact YAML format Bases requires, valid CSS property names, Templater vs Core Templates syntax. And it validates every output. Generic AI gets these formats wrong more often than you'd expect. Just give it a try and you will make the same painful experience that I had.

> [!question]- What if the output isn't perfect?
> Iterate. Every tiere - including the free one - allows follow-up prompts. Tell the AI what to change and it updates the output. Or use the guided wizard for a more structured approach.

> [!question]- What about my data/privacy?
> Your prompts are sent to the AI to generate output. The data is not stored beyond your session. See [here](https://leanstudio.sascha-kasper.com/privacy) for details.

> [!question]- Can I use it commercially?
> Yes, absolutely, contingent to Obsidian's policy. At the time of publishing this, Obsidian is free for personal and commercial use.

> [!question]- Do I need Obsidian installed?
> Not for Lean Obsidian Studio - it runs in your browser. However, you will need Obsidian to *use* the generated files.
