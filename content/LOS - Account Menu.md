# Account Menu

> [!image|float-r] Account Menu
> ![[LOS - Account Menu.webp|Account dropdown showing Power tier badge and menu options like Manage Subscription and Sign Out]]
 
Click your **tier badge** or **usage counter** in the top-right corner of the header to open the account menu.

You see options to:
- Manage Subscription
- Payment Settings (paid tiers only)
- Change Password
- Export My Data
- Delete Account
- Sign Out
## Manage Subscription
Opens a dialog where you can:
- Upgrade or downgrade your tier (Explorer, Pro, Power)
- Switch between monthly and yearly billing

Changes take effect immediately. If you downgrade, you keep your current tier until the end of the billing period.
## Payment Settings
Available for Pro and Power subscribers. Opens the Customer Portal directly to your payment method settings where you can:
- Update your payment method
- Cancel your subscription
## Change Password
Opens a dialog where you can set a new password. The same five rules from signup apply (see [[LOS - Getting Started]]).
## Export My Data

Downloads a JSON file containing:
- Your profile information
- Your complete usage history

This is a GDPR-compliant data export. The file downloads directly to your browser.

## Delete Account

A permanent, two-step process:
1. Click **Delete Account**
2. Confirm in the dialog that appears

This removes your profile, usage history, and authentication data. This action cannot be undone.

## Sign Out

Logs you out of Lean Obsidian Studio and deletes all session data.

## Password Reset

If you forgot your password:
1. Open the **Sign In** modal
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for the reset link
5. Set a new password (same five rules as signup - see [[LOS - Getting Started]])
